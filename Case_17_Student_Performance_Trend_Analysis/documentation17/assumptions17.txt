Assumptions

1. The number of students is entered by the user.
2. Each student has marks for exactly three tests.
3. Student names are entered as strings.
4. Test marks are entered as numbers.
5. The average is calculated using all three test marks.
6. Performance trend is determined by comparing the third test mark with the first test mark.
7. Higher third-test marks indicate an improving trend, lower marks indicate a declining trend, and equal marks indicate a stable trend.
