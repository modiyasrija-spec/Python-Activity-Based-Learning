Assumptions

1. The number of products is entered by the user.
2. Product names are entered as strings.
3. Stock quantities are entered as integers.
4. Opening stock, incoming quantity, dispatched quantity, and actual closing stock are expected to be non-negative.
5. Expected closing stock is calculated as opening stock + incoming quantity - dispatched quantity.
6. A negative discrepancy indicates a stock shortage.
7. Replenishment quantity is calculated for shortages only.
