1. The number of product records is entered as an integer.
2. Product IDs and names are entered as strings.
3. Product prices are entered as numbers.
4. Stock quantities are entered as integers.
5. Product IDs are compared exactly and are case-sensitive.
6. A product is considered low-stock when its stock quantity is less than five.
7. Each duplicated product ID is reported only once.
8. The program does not validate negative prices or stock quantities.
