Flowchart Logic

Start
↓
Input number of products
↓
Repeat for each product
↓
Input product details
↓
Calculate expected closing stock
↓
Calculate discrepancy
↓
Is expected stock negative?
├── Yes → Display invalid stock movement
└── No → Is discrepancy non-zero?
├── Yes → Display discrepancy and shortage replenishment
└── No → Display stock reconciled
↓
Store product details in a list of tuples
↓
Display inventory reconciliation report
↓
Stop
